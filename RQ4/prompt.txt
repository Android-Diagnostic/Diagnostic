Your task is to analyze the diagnostic information that can be derived ONLY from the runtime log evidence provided below.

Analyze the diagnosis along five fixed diagnostic-information dimensions. These dimensions are evaluation dimensions, not mutually exclusive classification labels.

1. identify the diagnostic information supported by the logs;

0 = No diagnostically useful information for this dimension is supported

1 = The logs provide relevant but general, partial, ambiguous, or weakly

2 = The logs provide specific diagnostic information that meaningfully
    narrows or clarifies the diagnosis.


Evaluate the following dimensions:


Failure Characterization:
What exactly went wrong? This includes a more specific description of the failure or its manifestation, such as an exception type, error code, timeout, invalid state, or missing object.


Fault Localization:
Where did the failure occur? This includes a specific library, component, service, API, function, or resource.


Cause / Trigger:
Why did the failure occur? This includes a condition, event, dependency, input, or state that caused or triggered the failure.


Execution / Runtime Context:
How or under what conditions did the failure occur? This includes execution history, runtime state, parameters, environmental conditions, or interactions that help explain or reproduce the failure.


Post-failure Behavior:
What happened after the failure? This includes retry, fallback, recovery, exception handling, or subsequent service degradation.
